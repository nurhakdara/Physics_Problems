# 10. Light Speed Measurement

Measure the speed of light using a microwave oven, a bar of chocolate (or slices of cheese), and a ruler.

The formula used is:

$$
c = f\lambda
$$

Where:

- $c$ = speed of light  
- $f = 2.45 \times 10^9 \, \text{Hz}$  
- $\lambda$ = wavelength of the microwaves  

The distance between two melted spots represents half of the wavelength:

$$
d = \frac{\lambda}{2}
$$

Therefore:

$$
\lambda = 2d
$$

Assume the measured distance between melted spots is:

$$
d = 6.1 \, \text{cm} = 0.061 \, \text{m}
$$

Then the wavelength is:

$$
\lambda = 2(0.061)
$$

$$
\lambda = 0.122 \, \text{m}
$$

Now calculate the speed of light:

$$
c = f\lambda
$$

$$
c = (2.45 \times 10^9)(0.122)
$$

$$
c \approx 2.99 \times 10^8 \, \text{m/s}
$$

Experimental value:

$$
c \approx 2.99 \times 10^8 \, \text{m/s}
$$

Accepted value:

$$
c_{\text{accepted}} = 3.00 \times 10^8 \, \text{m/s}
$$

Percentage error formula:

$$
\% \text{ error} =
\left|
\frac{c_{\text{exp}} - c_{\text{accepted}}}
{c_{\text{accepted}}}
\right| \times 100
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
=
\left|
\frac{2.99 \times 10^8 - 3.00 \times 10^8}
{3.00 \times 10^8}
\right| \times 100
$$

$$
\approx 0.33\%
$$

## Final Answer

$$
c \approx 2.99 \times 10^8 \, \text{m/s}
$$

$$
\% \text{ error} \approx 0.33\%
$$

The measured value is very close to the accepted speed of light, showing that the experiment provides a good approximation of the speed of light.
