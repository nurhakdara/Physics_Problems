## Given

Velocity vector:

v = (2i - 4j + k) m/s

Magnetic field vector:

B = (i + 2j - k) T

Particle: Proton

Charge of proton:

q = 1.6 × 10^-19 C

--------------------------------------------------

# Main Formula

F = q(v × B)

Where:

F = magnetic force vector  
q = electric charge of the particle  
v = velocity vector  
B = magnetic field vector  
× = cross product operation  

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 1 — Compute the Cross Product

v × B =

|  i    j    k  |
|  2   -4    1  |
|  1    2   -1  |

Expanding determinant:

= i[(-4)(-1) - (1)(2)]
- j[(2)(-1) - (1)(1)]
+ k[(2)(2) - (-4)(1)]

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 2 — Simplify Components

i-component:

(-4)(-1) - (1)(2)
= 4 - 2
= 2

j-component:

(2)(-1) - (1)(1)
= -2 - 1
= -3

Because of minus sign before j:

-(-3) = 3

k-component:

(2)(2) - (-4)(1)
= 4 + 4
= 8

Therefore:

v × B = 2i + 3j + 8k

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 3 — Find the Magnitude

|v × B|

= √(2² + 3² + 8²)

= √(4 + 9 + 64)

= √77

≈ 8.775

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 4 — Calculate Magnetic Force

|F| = q|v × B|

= (1.6 × 10^-19)(8.775)

≈ 1.40 × 10^-18 N

--------------------------------------------------

# Final Answer

|F| ≈ 1.4 × 10^-18 N
