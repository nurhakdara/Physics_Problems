# 5. Energy Stored by Charge in a Capacitor

## Given

Plate area:

S = 0.02 m²

Distance between plates:

d = 5 mm = 0.005 m

Voltage:

U = 500 V

Permittivity of free space:

ε₀ = 8.85 × 10^-12 F/m

--------------------------------------------------

# Part 1 — Capacitance of Capacitor

## Main Formula

C = ε₀S / d

Where:

C = capacitance  
ε₀ = permittivity of free space  
S = plate area  
d = distance between plates  

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 1 — Substitute Values

C = (8.85 × 10^-12 × 0.02) / 0.005

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 2 — Calculate

C = 3.54 × 10^-11 F

--------------------------------------------------

# Result

Capacitance:

C = 3.54 × 10^-11 F

--------------------------------------------------

# Part 2 — Energy Stored in Capacitor

## Main Formula

W = (1/2)CU²

Where:

W = stored energy  
C = capacitance  
U = voltage  

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 1 — Substitute Values

W = (1/2)(3.54 × 10^-11)(500)²

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 2 — Calculate

W = 4.43 × 10^-6 J

--------------------------------------------------

# Result

Stored energy:

W = 4.43 × 10^-6 J

--------------------------------------------------

# Part 3 — Electric Field Intensity

## Main Formula

E = U / d

Where:

E = electric field intensity  
U = voltage  
d = distance between plates  

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 1 — Substitute Values

E = 500 / 0.005

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 2 — Calculate

E = 1.0 × 10^5 V/m

--------------------------------------------------

# Result

Electric field intensity:

E = 1.0 × 10^5 V/m

--------------------------------------------------

# Part 4 — Force of Attraction Between Plates

## Main Formula

F = (1/2)ε₀E²S

Where:

F = attraction force  
ε₀ = permittivity of free space  
E = electric field intensity  
S = plate area  

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 1 — Substitute Values

F = (1/2)(8.85 × 10^-12)(1.0 × 10^5)²(0.02)

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 2 — Calculate

F = 8.85 × 10^-4 N

--------------------------------------------------

# Final Answers

Capacitance:

C = 3.54 × 10^-11 F

Stored energy:

W = 4.43 × 10^-6 J

Electric field intensity:

E = 1.0 × 10^5 V/m

Force between plates:

F = 8.85 × 10^-4 N
