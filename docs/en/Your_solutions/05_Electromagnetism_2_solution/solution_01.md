# 1. Gauss's Law

## Given

Point charge:

q = +2 C

Radius of spherical surface:

r = 1 m

Permittivity of free space:

ε₀ = 8.85 × 10^-12 C²/(N·m²)

--------------------------------------------------

# Main Formula

Gauss’s Law:

Φ = q / ε₀

Where:

Φ = electric flux  
q = enclosed electric charge  
ε₀ = permittivity of free space  

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 1 — Substitute Values

Φ = 2 / (8.85 × 10^-12)

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 2 — Calculate

Φ ≈ 2.26 × 10^11 N·m²/C

--------------------------------------------------

# Final Answer

Electric flux through the spherical surface:

Φ ≈ 2.26 × 10^11 N·m²/C
