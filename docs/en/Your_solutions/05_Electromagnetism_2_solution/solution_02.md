# 2. Ampere’s Law

## Given

Current in first wire:

I₁ = 5 A

Current in second wire:

I₂ = 5 A

Distance between wires:

d = 10 cm = 0.10 m

Point is midway between wires:

r = d/2 = 0.05 m

Permeability of free space:

μ₀ = 4π × 10^-7 T·m/A

--------------------------------------------------

# Main Formula

Magnetic field due to a long straight wire:

B = (μ₀I) / (2πr)

Where:

B = magnetic field  
μ₀ = permeability of free space  
I = electric current  
r = distance from wire  

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 1 — Magnetic Field from First Wire

B₁ = (4π × 10^-7 × 5) / (2π × 0.05)

Cancel π:

B₁ = (20 × 10^-7) / 0.1

B₁ = 2 × 10^-5 T

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 2 — Magnetic Field from Second Wire

Because the currents flow in opposite directions, the magnetic fields at the midpoint point in the same direction.

So:

B₂ = 2 × 10^-5 T

--------------------------------------------------

# Step 3 — Total Magnetic Field

B_total = B₁ + B₂

= 2 × 10^-5 + 2 × 10^-5

= 4 × 10^-5 T

--------------------------------------------------

# Direction of Magnetic Field

Using the right-hand rule:

- Magnetic field from first wire points downward at the midpoint.
- Magnetic field from second wire also points downward at the midpoint.

Therefore, the total magnetic field is downward.

--------------------------------------------------

# Final Answer

Magnitude of magnetic field:

B = 4 × 10^-5 T

Direction:

Downward (according to the right-hand rule)
